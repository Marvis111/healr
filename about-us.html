About Us

HealR App is part of MyZODA's portfolio of advanced digital platforms, built with the latest cloud, AI, and security technologies to deliver seamless healthcare experiences.

At HealR, we believe that quality healthcare should be within reach for everyone — no matter where you live or how busy life gets. We're building a simple, reliable telemedicine platform that connects real people to real doctors, without the stress of long hospital lines or unnecessary travel.

We're proudly backed by MyZODA - a company built with the heart and spirit of a Nigerian team that understand the struggles of our communities, but HealR is built with the heart and hustle of a Nigerian team that understands the daily struggles our communities face. From Lagos to Abuja, Port Harcourt to Kaduna — we know what it means when power goes out, when traffic steals half your day, or when a loved one needs help in the middle of the night. That's why we created a better way to get care.

With HealR, you can speak to trusted doctors, book appointments, get prescriptions, and manage your health — all from your phone.
